Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit

Indonesia's dry season (May to September) offers the best weather for most destinations. However, specific regions may have different optimal visiting times.

Visa Information

Many countries are eligible for visa-free entry to Indonesia for up to 30 days. Check the latest requirements before your trip.

Local Customs

Indonesia is diverse, with each region having unique customs. Dress modestly when visiting religious sites and always ask permission before taking photos of locals.

Culinary Adventures

Don't miss trying local specialties at each destination. Indonesian cuisine varies greatly from region to region, offering a feast for food lovers.
