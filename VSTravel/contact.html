Frequently Asked Questions

What are your office hours?

Our offices are generally open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Each branch may have slightly different hours, so please check the specific office information above.

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our customer service hotline.

Can I visit your office without an appointment?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome during our regular business hours. However, if you would like to meet with a specific travel consultant, we recommend making an appointment to ensure they are available.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer virtual consultations via video call. Please contact us via email or phone to schedule a virtual appointment with one of our travel experts.
