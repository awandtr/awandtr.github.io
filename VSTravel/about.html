Our History

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leader

2020: Foundation

VSTravel was established as a small travel agency in Jakarta by Vincent Santoso, with just 3 staff members and a passion for showcasing Indonesia's beauty to travelers.

2021: First Expansion

Despite the challenging pandemic period, we opened our second office in Medan to serve travelers in Northern Sumatra, focusing on domestic tourism recovery.

2022: Service Diversification

VSTravel expanded its offerings to include specialized cultural tours, adventure packages, and MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) services.

2023: National Recognition

Our commitment to quality earned us the "Outstanding Travel Service" award from Indonesia's Tourism Board and we opened our Surabaya branch office.

2024: Digital Transformation

VSTravel launched its comprehensive digital platform, embracing technology to enhance customer experience and reach global markets.

2025 & Beyond

Now with 50+ dedicated staff across four offices, VSTravel continues to innovate and set new standards in the Indonesian travel industry.
